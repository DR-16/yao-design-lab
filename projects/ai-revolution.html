一份经济作业, 我把它做成了能玩的游戏 An econ homework — I turned it into a game

Econ 12 期末给了 10 个选题, 我挑了第 10 个: AI 对加拿大经济的冲击. 老师给的形式是 "choose your own adventure". 大多数同学交 slides. 我把这句话当真了 — 做成了一个 14 分钟能玩完的分支游戏, 挂在永久 URL 上, 任何人打开链接就能玩。 Econ 12's final gave us 10 topics — I picked #10: AI's impact on Canada's economy. The brief literally said "choose your own adventure." Most classmates delivered slide decks. I took the wording seriously and built a 14-minute branching game, deployed to a permanent URL that any classmate or teacher can open.

v2.0 · 转折pivot PPT → 能玩的网站deck → playable site

已经在写 slides 了, 突然反应过来: 老师给的主题词就是这个. 那"用媒介本身回答问题"比用文字总结好一百倍。停掉 PPT, 改做互动网站 — 让玩家亲自做 4 个人生选择, 走完 2026 → 2035 十年时间线。 Was halfway into slides when it hit me — that phrase in the prompt was the whole point. Local Playwright + headless Chromium spits out 5 retina PNGs, ~3.8MB each, automated.

交完之后 · 学到的几件事 POST-SUBMISSION · WHAT I ACTUALLY LEARNED

一份作业教我的事 What one homework taught me

01

题目里的关键词, 就是它想让你用的形式 The prompt's own words are usually the answer format

"Choose Your Own Adventure" 是题目主题, 我却差点交 PPT. 后来才反应过来: 老师写这句话不是文学修辞, 是暗示 — 用这个形式回答, 分数封顶更高。读题的时候要把题目当 spec, 不是当 vibe。 "Choose Your Own Adventure" was in the prompt. I almost delivered slides anyway. When you finally notice, you realise the prompt wasn't decoration — it was a spec. The teacher named the medium; using it lifts the ceiling on your grade. Read prompts like specs, not vibes.

02

免费的 URL 比昂贵的 demo 更值钱 A free URL beats an expensive demo

GitHub Pages + 静态 HTML = 一年 $0, 老师同学家人陌生人都能点开就玩. 这个成本结构改变了所有后续项目的默认动作 — "能不能挂到 URL 上" 是 v0 的一部分, 不是 v2 才想的事。 GitHub Pages + static HTML = $0/year, and teachers, classmates, family, strangers all just click and play. That cost structure changed how every later Yao Design Lab project starts: "does it have a URL?" is a v0 question now, not a v2 afterthought.

03

一份产品 + 一份使用说明 = 交付 A product plus how-to-use-it equals a delivery

游戏链接是"作品", 5 张路径截图 + 讲稿 + 提交清单 是"说明"。老师不用现场点 5 遍就能看到"所有可能路径的完整走法". 这一步的差别就是"作品"和"作品集"的差别。 The game link is the artifact. The 5 path screenshots + speech scripts + delivery manifest are the manual. The teacher doesn't have to click through 5 times to see what every path feels like. That step is the difference between a product and a portfolio.

04

学校作业, 本来就可以被做到超出范围 School work can — and probably should — be over-delivered

大家默认交 10 页 Word 文档, 我交了一个能玩 14 分钟的网站 + 一份复现指南. 花的时间没多多少, 但这才是 16 岁的我唯一能不被 AI 替代的地方 — 做到超出被期待的形状。这份作业本身, 也回答了它自己的 essential question。 Everyone submits a 10-page Word doc. I submitted a 14-minute playable website plus a rebuild guide. Not that much more effort — but this is the one thing a 16-year-old can do that AI still can't: deliver in a shape no one was expecting. The homework, in doing so, answered its own essential question.
