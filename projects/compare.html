承诺一promise 1

准. Accurate.

不猜、不编、不会同一款车这次给你 3.0T 下次给你 3.6T。 No guessing, no fabricating, no telling you 3.0T today and 3.6T tomorrow for the same car.

具体做了什么 →what makes it that way →

  • 用 Groq · llama-3.3-70b-versatile, 对全球主流车型数据掌握得比通用聊天模型好Uses Groq · llama-3.3-70b-versatile, whose grasp of global car specs beats generic chat models
  • temperature: 0 — 强制确定性输出, 同样的输入永远得到同样的对比temperature: 0 — deterministic. Same query, same answer forever
  • response_format: json_object — 强制 JSON, 6 段结构不可能变形response_format: json_object — schema-enforced. The 6-section shape can't drift
  • System prompt 明确写"用专业知识填写、不要编造", 字段格式和地区都锁死System prompt explicitly says "use your knowledge, don't fabricate," locks field format and region

为什么长成这个样子: 推倒 5 次 Why it looks like this: 5 teardowns

6 版之后 · 学到的几件事POST-V6 · WHAT I ACTUALLY LEARNED

推倒 5 次 教我的事 What 5 teardowns taught me

01

"看起来最保险"通常是最贵的选择 The "safest-looking" option is usually the most expensive one

付费 API 稳, 本地模型免费, Railway 部署快 — 每一个"最保险"的选择, 都让我半个月后必须推倒重来. 保险是给下一次自己找罪受。 Paid API is "reliable," local models are "free," Railway is "fast to deploy" — each safe pick forced a teardown two weeks later. Safe now is expensive later.

02

"$0" 不是 nice-to-have, 是分享的前提 "$0" isn't nice-to-have — it's the prerequisite for sharing

只要作品每月扣我一分钱, 我在心里就会开始给它算账、纠结要不要停. 一旦纠结, 就没了"想让人一直用"的动力. $0 才让作品真正独立于我的心情之外。 The moment a project costs me a dollar a month, I start second-guessing whether to keep it up. That doubt kills the "I want people to keep using this" energy. $0 is what lets a project outlive my mood.

03

稳定的 95% > 波动的 99% A steady 95% > a jittery 99%

用户不是在测"最高准度", 是在判断"我能不能相信这个工具". 每次都错在同样的地方, 我知道怎么绕; 每次都错在不同地方, 就没人再用了。 Users don't benchmark peak accuracy — they gauge trustworthiness. Consistently wrong in the same spot, they route around it. Randomly wrong, they just leave.

04

好作品不能有"我"这个单点故障 A good project can't have "me" as a single point of failure

"我这台 Mac 开着" / "我记得续费" / "我这个月心情好" — 每有一个依赖是"我", 作品就多一个明天会挂的理由. 最终架构没有任何一步需要我在场。 "My Mac is on" / "I remembered to renew" / "I still care this month" — every dependency on me is a reason the project dies tomorrow. The final architecture needs me nowhere.
